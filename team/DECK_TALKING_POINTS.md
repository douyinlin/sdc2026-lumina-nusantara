## Slide 15

- 05 · EXPLAINABLE SCORING
- One score, five inputs — auditable to the source
- THE MODEL (AS SHIPPED IN THE DEMO)
- score = 0.30 · skill coverage + 0.25 · role focus + 0.15 · transferability + 0.15 · demand trend + 0.15 · (1 − gap cost)
- Fast-disruption scenario re-weights toward trend (0.35) and penalises flat or declining roles — the toggle is visible in the demo.
- Every input opens in the UI Each skill signal carries its source name, publish date and retrieval date. Scores are prototype estimates — never hiring probabilities.

## Slide 16

- 05 · HOW MATCHING WORKS — EVIDENCE TRAIL
- Relevant Matches, with a Clear Evidence Trail
- Inputs that drive matching Confirmed skills (CV parse or manual ticks) + target field
- Future-role match Ranked roles, each scored on five auditable inputs the UI shows: coverage · role focus · transferability · demand trend · gap cost
- Inputs that drive duration Weekly study hours → pathway length in weeks (e.g. "about 30 weeks at 6 h/week")
- Recorded only Years of experience, move urgency, training budget
- Every match carries its evidence — judges can trace each score
- Each role card names what was matched (skills already held vs. gaps to close, labelled important or not), the demand-trend source behind the score (dated 2025–2026 snapshots: DOSM online job offerings, a JobStreet–Randstad signal reported by The Edge, WEF Future of Jobs 2025, PIKOM 2025), and a scenario toggle that re-ranks under moderate vs. fast AI disruption.
- Stated honestly, including in the app itself
- The app states under the intake form: "Experience, urgency and budget are recorded only, not used in scores or course filtering." Scores are prototype estimates — weighted rules, not hiring probabilities; the app says so on every results page. Three synthetic CVs can demonstrate the matching logic and career-stage differences. They cannot prove statistical recommendation accuracy — that needs a larger sample and an independent evaluation.
- Three fictional CVs, one live site, zero invented numbers — every role, score and gap quoted in this insert is visible in the demo.
